# Testing Facts: The Marble Archive

Each section below is a small, distinct fact. Upload this file, then ask the
chat about any single fact — a correct answer proves the chunk with that
fact was retrieved. Facts are deliberately absurd so no model would "know"
them from training.

## The lighthouse keeper

The lighthouse keeper of Tromsø waters his one fern exclusively with cod
liver oil every Tuesday at dusk, and has done so for thirty-one years
without missing a single week, except during the great storm of 2003 when
the fern itself held the lamp glass steady.

## Purple mailboxes

In 1987 the town of Bumblefleet painted all 214 of its mailboxes purple to
honor a jazz trio that got lost driving through and stayed for the whole
summer festival. The mailboxes are still purple, repainted every June.

## The clockmaker's promise

The last mechanical clockmaker of Kittredge Hollow signed every repair
ticket with a small inked snail, promising customers the work would outlive
the bell that struck noon in the town square, which it always has.

## Underwater library

The flooded quarry beneath Millhaven holds a lending library in a repurposed
grain silo. Patrons swim down with waxed paperbacks and return them by the
next full moon. The most borrowed title is a cookbook, despite the absence
of any kitchen.

## The kite census

Every leap year, the village of Sarrow-on-Wren conducts a census of kites
grounded in its trees. The record year was 2019 with one hundred and
fourteen kites, of which forty-one were reclaimed, two were claimed by owls,
and the rest were left to become winter ornaments.

## Nomadic bench

The green bench outside the Sarrow post office relocates roughly nine meters
each spring. Nobody claims responsibility. The bench is always found facing
the direction the swallows departed the previous autumn.
